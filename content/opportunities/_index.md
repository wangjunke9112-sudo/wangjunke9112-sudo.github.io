        The LENS Lab is a new research group at South China University of Technology, working on semiconductor optoelectronic devices for energy and light technologies. We are recruiting motivated researchers at all levels.

        We are looking for people who want to help build something from the beginning rather than join an established platform. We particularly value curiosity, scientific rigor, hands-on ability, clear writing, and the willingness to work both independently and collaboratively.

  - block: markdown
    content:
      title: Postdoctoral Researchers
      text: |
        We welcome applications from highly motivated postdoctoral researchers interested in semiconductor optoelectronic devices for energy and light technologies.

        Research directions include multijunction photovoltaics, perovskite and related thin-film semiconductors, interface engineering, device physics, stability, and integrated optoelectronic systems. Candidates with backgrounds in perovskite solar cells, tandem photovoltaics, thin-film devices, optoelectronic characterization, interface chemistry, electrochemical systems, or related fields are encouraged to apply.

        Postdocs in the LENS Lab will be supported in developing independent research directions, writing proposals, building international collaborations, mentoring students, and preparing for future academic or industry careers.

        Several fully funded positions may be available depending on fit and timing.

    content:
      title: Graduate Students (PhD and Master)
      text: |
        We welcome applications from motivated graduate students interested in semiconductor materials, optoelectronic devices, and energy technologies.

        Students in the LENS Lab will receive training in materials processing, device fabrication, characterization, data analysis, scientific writing, and project development. Prior experience in perovskite devices is helpful but not required — strong candidates from adjacent fields such as materials chemistry, solid-state physics, electrical engineering, or electrochemistry are also welcome.

      title: Research Assistants
      text: |
        The LENS Lab may recruit research assistants to support laboratory setup, device baseline development, SOP preparation, literature surveys, and data organization. This role is suitable for candidates who wish to gain intensive research experience before applying to graduate programmes.

        Research assistant positions do not automatically guarantee admission to a future degree programme.
