abstract: "Two new wide-bandgap polythiophenes, i.e., poly[5,5\u2032-bis(2-hexyldecyl)-(2,2\u2032-bithiophene)-4,4\u2032-dicarboxylate-alt-5,5\u2032-3-chloro-2,2\u2032-bithiophene] (PDCBT-Cl) and poly[5,5\u2032-bis(2-hexyldecyl)-(2,2\u2032-bithiophene)-4,4\u2032-dicarboxylate-alt-5,5\u2032-3,3\u2032-dichloro-2,2\u2032-bithiophene] (PDCBT-2Cl) comprising 3-chloro-2,2\u2032-bithiophene and 3,3\u2032-dichloro-2,2\u2032-bithiophene moieties, respectively, were synthesized for fullerene-free polymer solar cells (PSCs). For comparison, three other polymers based on [2,2\u2032-bithiophene]-4,4\u2032-dicarboxylate (DCBT), i.e., PDCBT, PDCBT-F, and PDCBT-2F with 2,2\u2032-bithiophene, 3-fluoro-2,2\u2032-bithiophene, and 3,3\u2032-difluoro-2,2\u2032-bithiophene as comonomers, respectively, were also prepared. PSC devices were fabricated with these polymers as donor materials and ITIC-Th1 as acceptor. The incorporation of chlorine (Cl) or fluorine (F) atoms into polymers both efficiently downshifted the highest occupied molecular orbital (HOMO) energy levels, leading to higher open-circuit voltage (Voc) in the PSCs.
