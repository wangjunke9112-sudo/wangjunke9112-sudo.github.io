Lequan is a Master student in Materials Physics and Chemistry at South China University of Technology, working in Prof. Junke Wang's group on wide-bandgap perovskites for tandem solar cells. Her research focuses on stability-related challenges of wide-bandgap perovskite sub-cells, which remain one of the main barriers to high-performance, durable tandem photovoltaics.

Lequan earned her Bachelor degree in Materials Engineering from Anhui University of Technology, where she received training in materials processing and characterization. Her undergraduate research spanned solution-based preparation of mesoporous TiO₂, and for her thesis, the temperature effect on the microstructure and corrosion behaviour of stainless steel — building hands-on experience with material and electrochemical characterization along the way.

This grounding in how processing and microstructure shape material behaviour carries directly into her current work, where controlling thin-film formation processes, interfaces, and phase stability is central to the performance of perovskite optoelectronic devices.
