Junke Wang visited the Global Institute of Future Technology (GIFT) at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) as an invited speaker, hosted by Prof. Chen Hao.

![Invitation letter](featured.jpg)

The talk, titled Perovskite multijunction photovoltaics: materials, interfaces, and device platforms, gave an overview of recent developments in the field — from wide-bandgap perovskite films and their interfaces through to monolithic all-perovskite tandem devices. Rather than focusing on individual results, it shared a broader perspective on where multijunction technologies may be headed, and on the materials, interface, and stability challenges that will shape the next generation of high-efficiency thin-film photovoltaics.

The talk was followed by an engaged discussion on interface engineering, operational stability, and scalable fabrication. The visit was also something of a reunion, bringing together several former colleagues from the Sargent group who are now based at SJTU, and opened promising avenues for future exchange.

We thank Prof. Chen Hao and the Global Institute of Future Technology for the kind invitation and warm hospitality.
