Junke Wang visited the Global Institute of Future Technology (GIFT) at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) as an invited speaker, hosted by Prof. Chen Hao.

![Dr. Erkan Aydin and Dr. Junke Wang in the PV Fabrication Lab](featured.jpg)

The talk shared recent insights into multijunction photovoltaics — from wide-bandgap perovskite films and their interfaces through to monolithic all-perovskite tandem devices. Rather than dwelling on individual results, the talk stepped back to outline the LENS Lab's broader research vision and where multijunction technologies may be headed, including the materials, interface, and stability challenges that will shape the next generation of high-efficiency thin-film photovoltaics.

The presentation was followed by an engaged discussion with Prof. Chen's group on interface engineering, operational stability, and scalable fabrication, and opened promising avenues for future exchange between our groups.

We thank Prof. Chen Hao and the Global Institute of Future Technology for the kind invitation and warm hospitality.
