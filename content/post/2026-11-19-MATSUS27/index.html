We are co-organising a symposium, **"Multilayer Perovskite Optoelectronics: Materials, Interfaces, and Device Platforms,"** at MATSUS Spring 2027 (nanoGe), together with Dr. Lethy Krishnan Jagadamma (University of St Andrews) and Dr. Bin Chen (Northwestern University). The symposium brings together researchers working on multijunction and multilayer perovskite devices — spanning materials, interfaces, characterisation, and device platforms for photovoltaics and beyond. We warmly welcome abstract submissions and look forward to an engaging programme.
