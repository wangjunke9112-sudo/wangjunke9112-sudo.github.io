The LENS Lab — Light–Energy Nanosystems and Semiconductors — has officially opened at the School of Materials Science and Engineering and the State Key Laboratory of Luminescent Materials and Devices, South China University of Technology.

The group focuses on advanced semiconductor optoelectronic devices for energy and light technologies, with initial projects in multijunction photovoltaics, perovskite thin-film semiconductors, interface engineering, and device stability.

We are actively recruiting postdoctoral researchers and graduate students. Interested candidates are welcome to [get in touch](/opportunities/).
