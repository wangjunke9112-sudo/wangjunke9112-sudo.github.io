Our group has officially opened at the School of Materials Science and Engineering and the State Key Laboratory of Luminescent Materials and Devices, South China University of Technology.

We are interested in optoelectronic devices for energy and light technologies, with initial projects in multijunction photovoltaics, perovskite thin-film semiconductors, interface engineering, and device stability.

We are currently recruiting postdoctoral researchers, with several positions available to start immediately, and welcome master's and PhD students applying for the 2027 academic year. Interested candidates are encouraged to [get in touch](/opportunities/).
