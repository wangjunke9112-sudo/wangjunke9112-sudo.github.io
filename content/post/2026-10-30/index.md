Junke Wang gave an invited talk at the MATSUS26 Fall meeting (nanoGe) in Mallorca, Spain, presenting the group's recent work on multijunction perovskite optoelectronics and the loss mechanisms that limit multilayer device performance. It was a great chance to share our research directions and to reconnect with colleagues across the perovskite and emerging-semiconductor community.
